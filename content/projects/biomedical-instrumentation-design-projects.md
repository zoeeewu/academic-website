## EOG-Based Gaze-Directed Audio Enhancement System for Auditory Processing Disorder

- Developed an EOG-based gaze-directed audio enhancement system      using custom analog signal conditioning, and gaze classification and audio      control by LabVIEW. 
- Validated directional switching performance and quantified signal      decay and recalibration requirements to identify limitations for future      assistive-hearing applications.
