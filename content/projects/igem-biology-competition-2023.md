## iGEM Biology Competition (Student-led Research Team) 2023

## Engineered probiotic oral delivery drug for mitigating gluten intolerance

2023.01 – 2023.10

    Developed a synthetic biology strategy using genetically engineered probiotics to colonize the gut and degrade gluten before immune activation. Proposed an “immune-threshold” approach to reduce intestinal gluten immunogen load and attenuate downstream mucosal immune responses.

    Assessed key translational considerations, including biosafety and consumer acceptance, and consolidated outcomes into project documentation.
